Kopi & Roti Cafe

Welcome to Kopi & Roti Cafe, a cosy neighbourhood cafe in Kuala Terengganu. Our slogan is "Fresh coffee, warm bread, happy mornings." We bake our bread every day and brew our coffee fresh for every cup.

How to Order

  1. Choose your food and drinks from the menu board.
  2. Tell our cashier your order and your table number.
  3. Pay by cash, card or e-wallet.
  4. Wait at your table while we prepare your order.
