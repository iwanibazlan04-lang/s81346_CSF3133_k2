About the Event

TechFest 2026 is a one-day event organised by the Faculty of Computing on 15 November 2026. Join us for talks, hands-on workshops and a friendly hackathon.

Keynote Speaker: Dr. Nuh Ayden

Dr. Nuh Ayden is a senior software architect with more than 15 years of experience. She will share how modern software shapes our future.

Activity: Hackathon Challenge

Form a team of up to four members and build a working prototype in two hours. Exciting prizes are waiting for the best teams!
