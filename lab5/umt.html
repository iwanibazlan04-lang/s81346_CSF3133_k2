About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university is known for its focus on marine science, aquatic resources, science and technology.

UMT provides various academic programmes and opportunities for students to develop their knowledge, skills and leadership abilities. It is committed to academic excellence, research and innovation.

Through education and research, UMT aims to contribute to society and produce graduates who are knowledgeable, competent and responsible.
